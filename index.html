Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026 (2026-11-21)

This notice explains the Android game Classic Word Search, published under the LittleSteps Lab brand. The release package is com.littlesteps.classicwordsearch; development installations use a separate .debug package. Questions can be sent to littlesteps.surat@gmail.com.

In brief: saved puzzles, preferences and local best times stay on your device. Optional Google Play Games profiles and leaderboards are separate: when configured and signed in, the game sends limited completion scores to Google, not puzzle saves. There are no publisher-operated accounts, purchases, custom game servers or cloud puzzle synchronization. Google advertising, privacy messaging, Play Games and eligible Play update services can handle data off the device. “Offline gameplay” does not mean “no SDK data collection.”

Version scope: features depend on the installed app version. Builds distributed with Play Games disabled do not initialize its SDK. The Play Games information below applies only to supported builds where that feature is enabled and configured; it does not say that an upcoming update is already available.

Play Games deletion: manage or delete Classic Word Search's Google-hosted game data using Google's self-service controls. Your device-only puzzles and best times are separate.

The game uses a family-conscious design and is intended for players aged 3 and older, with no upper age limit. This audience statement is not an age-verification process or a legal minimum-age rule. Official content ratings are the ratings displayed by Google Play for the released version and region, determined through the IARC/store process; content ratings, target-audience declarations and Data Safety are separate. A parent or legal guardian should review this notice and the Terms of Use, decide whether the vocabulary, difficulty and advertising are suitable for their child, and supervise any use of other apps or websites.

The first-run Welcome screen is informational. It offers Start playing and Privacy Policy/Terms of Use readers, without age attestation or a Terms-agreement checkbox. Start playing dismisses the notice; only a completed/notice-seen flag and the policy revision (2026-11-21 for this notice) are saved locally. An updated policy revision can cause the notice to appear again. This is not a record of age, contract acceptance or consent. The Welcome screen does not collect an exact age, birth year, date of birth, identity document or app account, and does not verify any parental permission that applicable law may require.

All Google advertising requests receive child treatment for every player. This conservative configuration applies without asking the player's age. Neither an audience or rating declaration nor dismissing Welcome enables adult or personalized advertising. Google privacy settings are safeguards, not a certification that the app complies with every law in every country.

2. Information stored locally

The game uses a bundled English vocabulary and private Android app storage. Its versioned saved-game file, game-state.json, can contain:

  • Level progress, current puzzle boards, found words, completion state and elapsed playing time.
  • Local best times and the word indices that have received a first-letter hint.
  • Sound, music, light/dark theme, grayscale and grid-line preferences.
  • Completed-puzzle counters and timestamps used to space automatic full-screen ads.

The Welcome notice-seen flag and policy revision are also local; they remember notice dismissal, not an age declaration, agreement or consent. Google SDKs may keep their own privacy state, caches or technical information within the installation. The publisher has not added an app-owned online database, SQLite game database, custom backend, publisher-operated account/profile, billing or cloud puzzle synchronization. Optional Google-managed Play Games profiles and leaderboards are described in section 7.

The game does not upload saved boards, found/hinted word lists or preferences to a publisher server or supply them as ad-targeting fields. When the optional Play Games service is configured and authenticated, limited level/time scores are sent to Google as described below; this does not upload the saved-game file or synchronize puzzles. Local best-time records remain independent of Google's leaderboard entries.

Original nature scenery, letter tiles and brief celebrations are drawn locally with native Android views. The original music loop and sound effects are generated on the device, follow the local music/sound preferences, and respect foreground state and audio focus. This is playback, not microphone recording or collection of your music library; the game does not download scenery or audio tracks from a server.

3. Google advertising and off-device data

The app includes Google Mobile Ads SDK 25.5.0 for AdMob advertising. Google's SDK disclosure describes automatic collection and sharing for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention, including:

  • The device's IP address, which can be used to estimate a general or approximate location.
  • App and ad interactions, such as launches, taps and video views.
  • Diagnostic information about app/SDK performance, including loading time, hangs and energy usage.
  • Device or app identifiers, such as app set ID, and, where applicable, other identifiers described in Google's disclosure.

The app sets AgeRestrictedTreatment.CHILD, a maximum Google ad-content rating of G, and PublisherPrivacyPersonalizationState.DISABLED before initializing the ads SDK. Google's child-treatment documentation says personalized ads and remarketing are disabled, third-party ad-vendor requests are restricted, and the Android Advertising Identifier (AAID) is not transmitted. The app also removes the Google AD_ID permission. These settings do not establish that all other identifiers, IP addresses, diagnostics or ad-interaction data disappear. The G setting is an ad filter, not an official content rating awarded to this game.

The publisher does not send a player name, date of birth, email address, account profile or saved puzzle as advertising input. Google handles SDK server data under its own policies, which describe possible processing on servers outside your country. An advertisement you choose to open can lead to an advertiser's website or another app with its own data practices.

4. Privacy messaging and choices

Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) 4.0.0 checks with Google which privacy requirements and messages apply. Such requests involve off-device communication and request metadata; any applicable privacy state or choices are handled through Google's messaging service and local SDK storage. This app does not configure a consent-sync account identifier.

UMP is configured with its under-age-of-consent flag set to true for every player. Google documents that this flag prevents UMP from requesting consent from an under-age user. It is separate from the ads SDK's child-treatment setting; both are configured. A European advertising consent form therefore may not appear. No form appearing does not mean that Google services handle no data.

When Google reports that a privacy-options entry point is required, the app exposes Privacy options in Settings. A parent or guardian can help review the options made available there. Availability depends on Google's requirements and message configuration. Changing available privacy choices does not switch off this app's child-treatment protections or enable personalized ads.

Ad requests require the app's Google privacy-readiness checks to permit them. If those checks or ad requests fail, ordinary gameplay remains available. Dismissing the informational Welcome notice is not advertising consent or a record of permission for SDK data processing. Choosing a hint video is not a general consent to data collection.

Updated builds can retry failed privacy-setup requests after a delay while the game is in the foreground. A successfully returned privacy choice is not reset merely to retry a failed request or obtain an ad. Retries can involve further communication with Google; they do not relax child-treatment settings or guarantee that ads become available.

5. Where ads appear and how hints work

  • Banner: a bottom adaptive banner in a separate area outside the puzzle controls.
  • Interstitial: an automatic full-screen ad may appear at a completed-puzzle results screen after every five completed puzzles, subject to a minimum five-minute interval since the last full-screen ad. A rewarded video also updates that timestamp, delaying the next automatic interstitial. Inventory, privacy status and foreground readiness can cause an eligible ad to be skipped.
  • Optional rewarded video: the player explicitly chooses a video for one first-letter hint for an unfound, not-already-hinted word in the originating puzzle. The app grants the hint only when Google's SDK reports the reward as earned. Merely starting, closing or failing to load a video is not an earned reward. The hint does not solve the word, has no real-money value and cannot be bought or transferred.

The app does not show app-open, launch, exit or background ads. Ordinary gameplay does not require choosing a rewarded video. Google may receive ad-request data when ads are requested or preloaded while the app is in the foreground, even if the player never chooses a hint. The optional video is not a universal opt-out from banner, interstitial or SDK processing. Ad availability, content filtering and Google's review or serving decisions are not guaranteed by this notice.

Updated builds show loading, no-fill or network/unavailable status for hint videos and can retry failed or expired ad loads after bounded delays while in the foreground. A retry, ready status or dismissed ad does not itself earn a hint, and no retry promises that Google will supply a video.

6. Optional Google Play updates

Eligible Google Play installations use Play In-App Updates 2.1.0. The app can check update availability on foreground entry or when you choose Check for updates. A flexible update uses Google's confirmation/download flow while the app remains usable; installation/restart requires your explicit action and a safe saved-game state.

Google's update disclosure lists device metadata, application version, and the list of installed modules and asset packs, used to determine update availability and download size. This describes the app's installation components, not an app feature that reads all your other installed apps. The game does not supply saved puzzle content to the update check. Google says update data is encrypted, is not transferred to third parties, and is deleted after a fixed retention period; the disclosure does not specify that period's length.

Updates depend on Google Play availability, ownership and an eligible version. Local debug builds and unsupported installations cannot use the release package's Play update flow. There is no custom APK downloader or installer.

7. Optional Google Play Games profiles and leaderboards

Supported builds where enabled integrate Google Play Games Services v2, SDK 22.1.0, for Google-managed gaming identity and Levels, Easy, Medium and Hard leaderboards. This is not a publisher-created account or custom backend. Ordinary puzzles and local best times work without sign-in or a network connection. If Play Games is disabled or this installation lacks the required real configuration, its Play Games SDK is not started; the app does not show a fictitious profile or ranking.

On an enabled, supported and configured installation, the app initializes the SDK at application startup behind its configuration guard. Google's SDK can attempt authentication automatically, reuse an existing Google-managed Play Games profile, or offer Google's profile/name/avatar setup when needed. The compact sign-in action uses Google's flow when the player is not authenticated; a new installation does not necessarily create a new username or show a registration prompt. Google manages profile setup, account choices and applicable parental approval. Google's guidance allows eligible supervised accounts to create profiles with a parent's approval, but not every game or account supports supervised automatic sign-in. Cancellation, offline use or ineligible accounts must not prevent ordinary local play; successful authentication is not guaranteed.

Google profile creation/sign-in and the game's authenticated profile/score features are optional. You can decline or cancel Google's flow and keep playing local puzzles. An existing profile may sign in automatically according to Google's account settings; those settings also provide sign-out controls. Until authentication succeeds, the game does not request the current Player record or submit completion scores. Enabled builds can still initialize the SDK before Welcome or a sign-in action, and Google's documented automatic analytics/diagnostics are separate from these optional profile/score features. Declining Play Games does not turn off unrelated advertising or all SDK technical processing.

The app reads only the authenticated Player's display name for temporary on-screen display. It does not read a player ID, avatar URI or image, download or display an avatar, persist the profile, request an email address or request a friends list. This local display-name read is not itself an upload. Separately, Google's SDK collects gamer identity, including gamertag and avatar, when a Google Games account is created or updated. This confirmed SDK collection still applies even though app-owned code reads only the display name. Google manages the optional profile setup, avatar-selection/appearance metadata, profile visibility and stored service data; this is not an app photo-picker or collection of your device's photos. The app does not pass Play Games identity or scores into advertising requests or ad targeting.

No publisher-operated account does not mean no Google account/profile creation. Google's own profile-creation flow can be offered through the game's SDK, or an existing Google-managed profile can be used. Google controls that identity and its credentials. The game creates no separate publisher account, password, identity database or cloud puzzle save.

While Play Games is configured, authenticated and the game is in the foreground, a newly recorded puzzle completion automatically attempts a score submission for that player, without a separate confirmation for each score:

  • Levels: the actual current unlocked level after the completion is recorded locally; higher numeric levels rank better.
  • Easy, Medium and Hard: the actual elapsed milliseconds of that completed Classic puzzle, sent only to its matching difficulty leaderboard; lower times rank better.

These submissions do not include puzzle boards, words, saved-game files, preferences, purchases or custom analytics events. The app does not keep a deferred score-upload queue or upload old local results merely because sign-in or connectivity returns. A failed submission does not erase the local result or prove that Google accepted a score. Google controls its own SDK caching and service storage. A signed-in profile or a visible leaderboard is not proof of cloud saving or puzzle synchronization. The game does not use Saved Games, Recall or Google Drive to store or restore puzzle progress.

Google provides the leaderboard interface and profile-visibility controls. Depending on the player's Google settings, gaming identity and activity may be visible to other players, friends or the public; even a private profile can reveal that its display name is in use. Parents and players should review those choices and Google's automatic sign-in settings. See Play Games profile privacy and settings and Play Games SDK data disclosure. Google may also handle gaming activity under separate Play History settings; the game's fixed non-personalized ad configuration does not control every other Google service.

Manage / delete Play Games data — Classic Word Search

Google-hosted data and account controls: open your official Google Play Games profile to manage your profile or delete Classic Word Search game data. On builds with Play Games configured, Settings offers Manage / delete Play Games data, which opens that same Google page. It opens a self-service destination; it does not delete anything automatically or confirm that deletion succeeded. Google may ask you to sign in to the relevant Google account. A parent or guardian should help children review the choices.

Delete only Classic Word Search's Google-hosted game data

  1. Open https://play.google.com/games/profile in your browser, directly or using the configured Settings action.
  2. Choose Your Data → Delete Play Games Account & Data.
  3. Under Delete individual game data, find Classic Word Search and choose Delete, following Google's confirmation steps.

On Android, Google's Play Store route is Profile icon → Settings → General → Play Games Profile → Privacy & Settings → Delete profile & game data → Delete individual game data; select Classic Word Search. Menu names can change. See Google's official Android and web deletion instructions.

Entire shared profile and broader Google account controls

Deleting an individual game's data is different from deleting your entire shared Play Games profile. The latter can remove your gamer name, avatar/profile information, followers and other games' associated data; do not choose it merely to remove Classic Word Search data. Review Google's profile-deletion option and its consequences on the Play Games profile page. Google MyAccount provides broader Google-account controls; deleting a whole Google account is not required to remove only this game's data.

Local progress and other records remain separate

Local puzzles, found/hinted words, levels, preferences and personal best times remain in this installation independently of Google-hosted scores. Google's deletion controls do not clear the local game-state.json file; Android's Clear storage/uninstall controls below remove device-only progress. This game has no cloud puzzle save to restore it.

App-owned code does not persist an account, Play Games token or profile; it keeps only a temporary authenticated display-name label while running. Google's SDK, Android and your browser may manage their own authentication/cache state. Google's profile/sign-in settings can be used to manage future participation; signing in and playing again may generate new Google-held game activity. Deleting Play Games data does not automatically delete Google's separate Play History.

These are Play Games controls, not a promise to erase all third-party records. They do not give this publisher control over Google's advertising/UMP logs or another provider's retention. We cannot erase those records ourselves or promise a fixed deletion period. For provider-specific requests and retention, review Google's Privacy Policy and Google's own controls. Requests about support correspondence the publisher controls use the separate support-data route below.

8. Sharing and contacting support

Share opens Android's native chooser. The prepared text contains only the original app name or actual puzzle-result details, such as level/difficulty, grid size, words found and elapsed time. It does not automatically include a Play Games identity. Another app receives it only when you select a destination. There is no automatic send, contact upload or in-game chat. Optional Google-managed profiles/leaderboards are separate from this sharing action. A receiving app may transmit or retain the text under its own policy; check the destination and recipients, with a guardian's help where appropriate.

Contact support in Settings opens a native email app addressed to littlesteps.surat@gmail.com. It prefills only a subject containing the app name and version. It supplies no message body, attachments, logs, puzzle progress or profile details. You choose what to write and whether to send; the game does not send an email automatically or report that opening the email app means a message was sent. If no email app can be opened, the game displays the address and explains that nothing was sent.

If you send support email, you voluntarily provide your email address, message and any attachments you choose. These are handled through the support mailbox to respond to your request and address app or privacy concerns, not collected by a publisher-account form. Include only necessary details; guardians should contact support for children and avoid sending a child's full name, birth date, photos or other unnecessary personal information. You may ask about deletion of support correspondence, but provider retention, backups and applicable legal requirements can affect what can be removed.

9. Permissions and connectivity

The app declares Internet and network-state access for Google services. Its Google AD_ID permission is explicitly removed. Bundled Google/Android libraries can additionally declare normal advertising-services permissions and SDK service-management permissions; removal of AD_ID alone is not removal of every advertising-related capability.

The game does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone, broad file/media access or notification runtime permissions. It has no reminder or notification feature. Approximate location inferred by Google from an IP address does not require a location permission. Gameplay and reading bundled legal notices do not require a network connection; ads, privacy-message checks, Play Games authentication/leaderboards, external links, email and Play updates can use connectivity and your provider's data allowance.

10. Retention, removal and your choices

Request deletion of information the publisher controls: email littlesteps.surat@gmail.com and say the request concerns Classic Word Search. This includes support correspondence you chose to send, subject to the provider-retention and legal limitations described above. Do not send passwords, identity documents or unnecessary child information. For device-only progress use Android's removal controls below; for Google-held Play Games data use Google's separate controls. The publisher cannot directly erase another service's records or promise a fixed Google retention period.

Local progress and preferences remain in that installation until replaced by gameplay or removed. To reset them, use Android Settings → Apps → Classic Word Search → Storage (or Storage & cache) → Clear storage/Clear data, or uninstall the app. Menu names vary. Clearing cache alone is not the same as clearing saved-game data. Removal loses local progress, best times, hints, preferences and the Welcome notice-seen state; the publisher has no cloud save from which to recover them. A separate debug installation has separate local data.

The app disables Android app backup and excludes its app data from configured cloud backup and device-transfer rules. These are app configuration choices, not a guarantee about every device manufacturer's behavior or copies made outside the app.

Clearing storage or uninstalling removes app-local data from the selected installation through Android. It does not erase a Google Play Games profile or submitted leaderboard scores, other data already handled on Google's servers, support email, text sent to another app, operating-system records, or copies outside the app. Google's SDK/server retention and deletion are controlled by Google, not by the game's local save file. There is no publisher-operated player-account database or cloud puzzle save to delete.

The prominent Play Games data section above provides Google's self-service web/Android routes for Classic Word Search, explains the separate entire-profile choice, and links Google account controls. Those actions do not remove local puzzle files, publisher-controlled support correspondence or separate Google Play History.

For Google's broader controls, retention information and privacy requests, consult Google's Privacy Policy. Contact support for questions about information the publisher actually controls; we cannot promise deletion of another service's records or a fixed Google retention period.

These provider-specific statements are not a guarantee that every device, SDK channel, external share destination or service is risk-free, or that local data is end-to-end encrypted.
