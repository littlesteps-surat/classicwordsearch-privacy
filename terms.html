Advertising helps support the free game. The approved placements are:

4. The optional first-letter reward

Before a hint video, the app describes the reward and asks whether you want to watch. One earned SDK reward grants one first-letter hint for an unfound word that has not already been hinted in the originating puzzle. It highlights that word's starting letter/cell; it does not mark the entire word as found or solve it for you.

The hint is granted only from Google's earned-reward callback, not simply because a video started, a loading request succeeded or an ad was dismissed. Leaving before the SDK reports a reward may mean no hint. Loading/show failures do not create a reward. If the originating puzzle or an eligible word is no longer available, the game cannot apply that hint.

A hint is a non-transferable local game benefit. It is not money, a voucher or a prize with real-world value; it cannot be bought, sold, transferred to another player or installation, or redeemed for cash. There is no purchase, billing, gambling or cash-out feature. Removing local app data also removes saved hints.

The SDK controls the ad and its reward event. The publisher cannot guarantee that a video will be available or that every attempt will earn a reward. Choosing a hint video is not consent to personalized ads or a general waiver of privacy rights.
